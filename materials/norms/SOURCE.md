# ANPW_R affective norms — provenance

`ANPW_R-datasheet-1.xlsx` and `ANPW_R-datasheet-2.DOCX` are the supplementary data sheets of:

Imbir, K. K. (2016). Affective Norms for 4900 Polish Words Reload (ANPW_R): Assessments for
Valence, Arousal, Dominance, Origin, Significance, Concreteness, Imageability and, Age of
Acquisition. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 7, 1081. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.01081>

Published under the Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY) license and redistributed here, with
attribution, for reproducibility of the stimulus norming (`scripts/build_norms.R`).
